Chinmay Mysore Dharmendra

Ph.D. candidate, Department of Mathematics, University of Connecticut

I am a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at the University of Connecticut, advised by Tom Roby. I work in algebraic combinatorics, especially dynamical algebraic combinatorics and differential posets. My dissertation is on the combinatorics of Okada arc diagrams.

Before coming to UConn, I received my B.Math. and M.Math. from the Indian Statistical Institute, Bangalore.

I will be graduating with my Ph.D. in May 2027.

Research interests

  • Dynamical algebraic combinatorics (homomesy, combinatorial dynamics)
  • Representation theory and differential posets
  • Pattern avoidance and Catalan objects
  • Computational and experimental combinatorics

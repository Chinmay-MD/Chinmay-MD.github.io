Algebraic combinatorics

Current projects

Homomesy of linear feedback shifts

Homomesy is the phenomenon where a statistic has the same average over every orbit of a map. I study homomesy for masked and affine Fibonacci and Galois linear feedback shift maps. (Joint with M. Plante and T. Roby.)

Okada arc diagrams and the Young–Fibonacci lattice

The Young–Fibonacci lattice is a differential poset, a cousin of Young's lattice of partitions. My dissertation studies Okada arc diagrams and the Hivert–Scott RSK-type bijection associated with this lattice.
